## Brand & Style

This design system translates the vigor of clean athletic fuel into a refined editorial commerce experience. Designed for discerning wellness consumers, high-performance athletes, and health-conscious professionals, the aesthetic balances modern minimalism with tactile, high-energy vitality.

Key visual attributes:
- **Vital Minimalist**: Crisp, disciplined layouts anchored by deliberate negative space, avoiding cluttered fitness cliches in favor of apothecary-grade precision.
- **Organic Athleticism**: Deep arboreal tones paired with radiant, energetic terracotta accents signal both clean nutritional integrity and immediate kinetic power.
- **High-Trust Conversion**: Structural transparency achieved via clean card framing, explicit ingredient callouts, and prominent clinical/social validation markers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic yet aggressive dynamic, pitting botanical depth against catalytic warm energy.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**: Base background is set to warm off-white `#FAF9F6`, delivering warmth superior to clinical stark white. Elevated cards utilize `#FFFFFF` with structural borders in `#E8E5DF`.
- **Primary Botanical (`#1B4332`) & Secondary Forest (`#2D6A4F`)**: Used for branding headers, structural navigation, trust badges, and deep contrast panels. Reflects dense chlorophyll and wholesome plant sourcing.
- **Conversion Coral / Terracotta (`#E05A47`, interactive hover `#C84B39`)**: Reserved strictly for high-priority conversion paths, purchase triggers, alert banners, and active indicators. Never dilute its impact by using it as a passive background.
- **Sage Tint Highlight (`#E8F0EC`)**: Functional highlight layer applied to secondary badges, micro-interactions, active filter pills, and product macro callouts.
- **Typography & Neutrals**: Primary text sits at crisp `#1A1A1A` for maximum readability. Secondary and metadata typography utilizes `#595959`.

## Typography

Typography establishes an assertive architectural posture through **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and purposeful, neutral clarity through **Inter** for all narrative and operational elements.

- **Display & Headings**: Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans with tighter tracking (-0.01em to -0.03em) to produce a cohesive visual stamp. Weight remains firmly between bold (700) and extra-bold (800) to reinforce energy and athletic momentum.
- **Body & Product Narrative**: Rendered in Inter with generous line-height ratios (1.5–1.6) to guarantee legibility across detailed nutritional manifests, ingredient breakdowns, and clinical trial results.
- **Labels, Badges, & Metrics**: Small caps and tightly spaced bold Inter variants drive rapid visual categorization across nutritional spec panels (e.g., "30G PROTEIN", "0G ADDED SUGAR").

## Layout & Spacing

The system leverages a fluid-responsive layout grounded on an 8pt modular cadence.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid, max content boundary of 1280px, `margin` of 3rem, and `gutter` of 1.5rem. Split screen PDPs (Product Display Pages) distribute visual assets (7 columns) against purchase conversion mechanisms (5 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid, `margin` of 2rem, `gutter` of 1.25rem.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column grid, `margin-mobile` of 1.25rem, `gutter-mobile` of 1rem. Conversion buttons anchor persistently to the bottom safe area with zero side-padding bleed when docked.
- **Vertical Spacing & Stacking**: Section rhythm maintains generous padding (`space-xl` scaled dynamically to 4rem-6rem between macro sections) to evoke premium brand confidence and unhurried visual intake.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates via physical layering rather than diffuse blur rings, pairing delicate structural outlines with ambient botanical-tinted shadows.

- **Surface Baseline**: The foundational `#FAF9F6` plane acts as Level 0.
- **Card Framing**: Elevated components sit atop crisp 1px borders colored `#E8E5DF`. This prevents optical bleed between white surfaces and the warm off-white canvas.
- **Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are low-opacity, wide-dispersion, and slightly tinted with forest green to maintain an organic, grounded physical reality:
  - *Standard Card Rest*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04)`
  - *Interactive Card Hover*: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`
  - *Floating Action Elements*: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(224, 90, 71, 0.25)` (used exclusively for sticky coral conversion triggers)
- **Modal & Drawer Overlays**: `#1A1A1A` at 40% opacity with a subtle backdrop blur (`4px`) to focus intent on cart states and checkout flows.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances hyper-ergonomic pills for human touchpoints with structured geometry for informational architecture.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Applied to micro-containers, input fields, and quantitative selector controls.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-2xl` / `1rem` - `1.5rem`)**: Applied universally to product display units, ingredient cards, and review containers, conveying an approachable, soft tactile feel.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full` / `9999px`)**: Reserved for actions and badges: primary buttons, lifestyle filter tags, discount tags, and active state indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Conversion)**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#E05A47` background, white label typography (`label-lg`). Hover transition changes fill to `#C84B39` accompanied by `translate-y-[-1px]` and coral glow elevation.
- **Secondary Action**: Pill-shaped, transparent fill with a 1.5px `#1B4332` border and `#1B4332` text. Hover shifts background to `#E8F0EC`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Text-only using Plus Jakarta Sans bold with an animated underline indicator in forest green.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Attribute Badges (e.g., "Non-GMO", "30g Protein")**: High-contrast, compact pills. Neutral state uses `#E8F0EC` fill with `#1B4332` text (`label-sm`).
- **Urgency / Promotion Badges**: Pill-shaped, `#1B4332` surface with high-contrast coral or pure white typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E8E5DF`, border radius `0.5rem`. Focused state transitions to 1.5px border `#2D6A4F` with an unobtrusive sage glow ring.
- **Quantity Adjuster**: Pill-shaped segmented container (`#FAF9F6`), integrated decrement/increment controls, centered monospace/inter count.

### Product & Review Cards
- **Product Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E5DF`, radius `rounded-2xl`. Features an inset soft sage stage (`#E8F0EC`) for product imagery, high-contrast headline, nutrient micro-strip, and an integrated bottom primary CTA.
- **Social Proof / Reviews**: Crisp card layout featuring 5 filled star glyphs in `#1B4332`, a "Verified Buyer" pill chip, quote snippet in `body-md`, and product variant tag at the base.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selection
- **Subscription Frequency Toggle**: Dual-panel radio cards with selected states highlighted by a 2px `#1B4332` border, a light `#E8F0EC` tinted fill, and an absolute "Save 15%" badge perched on the top-right border.
